## Poster 1

Use case: ads-marketing. Create ONE finished Chinese App Store iPhone promotional poster, portrait canvas 1320 × 2868 pixels (aspect ratio 110:239). Image 1 is the REAL app screenshot and is the mandatory central visual, not a loose UI inspiration. Keep its content, Chinese text, icons, layout and proportions faithful; do not invent or redesign UI. Brand: 口袋相机助手. Refined minimalist flat blue and pale ice-blue / white palette matching the screenshot, no background gradient, no glassy decorations, no excessive 3D objects. Poster design: top 22% is beautiful editorial typography and generous whitespace; below, one large upright straight-on screenshot card occupying about 76% of canvas width, with a subtle thin dark-blue rounded device outline and restrained soft shadow. Keep the entire source screenshot visible inside, no tilt or perspective. Top small brand text exactly “口袋相机助手”. Large bold two-line headline exactly “让回忆更轻” and “给精彩留点空间”. Smaller subtitle exactly “照片、视频与 Live Photo，一起轻松压缩”. Background solid very pale blue. Tiny flat blue circle motif near one edge, restrained and optional. No English app name, no App Store badge, no fabricated metrics, no pricing, no added feature badges. Preserve original approved blue shutter-and-arrows app icon wherever present. Deliver a single high-resolution polished poster, no contact sheet, no extra margins outside canvas.
